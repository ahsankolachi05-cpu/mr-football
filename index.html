About the academy

Welcome to MR Football Coaching Academy

MR Football Coaching Academy is dedicated to giving young players a genuine next step after school — a full-time environment where serious football development sits side by side with real education.

Based at Southam United FC, the programme is built for players aged 16–19 who want more than weekend football: structured coaching through the week, competitive fixtures, individual development reviews and a Level 3 qualification that keeps every door open — on the pitch and beyond it.

Whether the goal is senior football, a professional club opportunity or university, every player follows a clear, supported pathway.
